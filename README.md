Crypto is still under pressure on Wednesday, November 11. The last update I gave you was from November 6, so that was five days old, not today's news.

**Prices**
- Bitcoin is around $73,600 after briefly dipping toward $72,700 overnight. Ethereum is near $2,050, XRP around $1.06 and Solana close to $83.
- Bitcoin's overnight low of $72,727 was its lowest since August, and buyers stepped in just above that level.
- Bitcoin is about 41% below its all-time high of $126,080 and closed below its 50-week moving average for the first time since September.
- The Fear & Greed Index is at 29, down from 32.

**Why it's down**
- October inflation came in hotter than expected, pushing traders to price in a possible Fed rate hike.
- That coincided with about $724 million in crypto liquidations, nearly 79% of them longs.
- US spot Bitcoin and Ether ETFs both had net outflows on November 9, their third straight session of outflows. Over the five sessions to November 10, Bitcoin ETFs lost about $1.06 billion and Ether ETFs about $326 million.
- Nvidia fell another 5.5% amid an investigation involving Jensen Huang, which is also weighing on risk appetite.

**Other news**
- The SEC cleared Nasdaq ISE to list options on funds holding qualifying digital commodities, without a new filing for each product.
- Miami-Dade County has started accepting bitcoin for property tax payments.
- Crypto is waiting on the outcome of Strategy's index decision tonight.

**Levels to watch**
- Support is at $72,700–$73,000. A sustained recovery would first need to reclaim $74,500–$75,000.
